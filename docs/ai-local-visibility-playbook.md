# ZB Captures Local Visibility Playbook

This is the off-site half of getting ZB Captures recommended more often by ChatGPT, Google AI, Bing/Copilot, and other assistant-style search tools.

## 1. Keep the brand single-purpose

- Use `ZB Captures` primarily as a real estate photography brand on the public web.
- Keep the same business name, phone, email, and city everywhere.
- When possible, use `Pensacola real estate photography` and nearby city phrases naturally in titles, descriptions, and profile copy.

## 2. Finish and maintain local business profiles

- Complete Google Business Profile with a real estate photography-focused description, service area, hours, phone, website, and fresh listing photos.
- Claim and complete Bing Places for Business with the same NAP details.
- Add the website to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Use the same short business summary across profiles so the entity stays consistent.

## 3. Ask for better reviews, not just more reviews

Ask happy clients to mention:

- the city
- the service they booked
- what it felt like to work with you

Good review themes:

- `Pensacola real estate photography`
- `drone photos`
- `easy booking`
- `quick delivery`
- `listing-ready photos`

SMS template:

```text
Hey [Name], thanks again for booking ZB Captures. If the photos helped and you have a minute, would you mind leaving a quick review? It helps a lot when agents mention the city, the kind of shoot, and what it was like working together.
```

Email template:

```text
Subject: Quick favor for ZB Captures

Thanks again for trusting me with the listing media. If the shoot and delivery were helpful, a short review would mean a lot. The most useful reviews usually mention the city, the type of coverage, and what stood out about the process.
```

## 4. Build third-party mentions

Prioritize citations and links from places that make sense locally:

- brokerage vendor pages
- agent team resource pages
- local chambers of commerce
- local business directories
- builder or stager partner pages
- association member profiles

The goal is not random backlinks. The goal is repeated local confirmation that ZB Captures is a real Pensacola-area real estate media business.

## 5. Publish and share the right pages

When someone asks for a referral, send:

- the city-specific location page if the listing city has one
- `/results.html` when they want proof
- `/trust.html` when they want process and reliability
- `/feedback.html` when they want social proof

## 6. Weekly operating rhythm

- Ask 2 to 3 recent clients for reviews.
- Add or improve 1 location page, case study, or proof page.
- Upload fresh photos to Google Business Profile.
- Share 1 useful page with a brokerage, agent, or partner who might refer work.

## 7. Avoid these traps

- Do not buy reviews.
- Do not stuff city names unnaturally.
- Do not publish generic AI-written pages with no real examples.
- Do not mix unrelated creative services into the main real estate positioning.
